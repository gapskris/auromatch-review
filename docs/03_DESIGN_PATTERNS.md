# Design Patterns: Tile Match & Mahjong Category

---

## Pattern 1: Reverse Construction Solvability
* **Observed in:** High-end puzzle design; absent in predatory ad-driven games.
* **Classification:** Potentially Distinctive
* **Purpose:** Ensures the generated puzzle is 100% winnable without requiring paid boosters.
* **Potential Benefit:** Establishes complete player trust; removes artificial paywalls.
* **Potential Implementation:** Populate tile coordinates backwards from an empty board state, assigning pairs/triads to exposed slots.
* **Status:** **ADOPTED**

---

## Pattern 2: Dedicated Senior View Mode (`[Aa]` Toggle)
* **Observed in:** *Vita Mahjong*
* **Classification:** Moderately Common in accessibility apps, rare in mainstream games.
* **Purpose:** Enlarges tile size by $+25\%$ and dims occluded background tiles by $50\%$.
* **Potential Benefit:** Solves eye strain and macular degeneration fatigue for older adults.
* **Potential Implementation:** Dynamic canvas zoom and luminosity filter toggled via a clean header button.
* **Status:** **ADOPTED**

---

## Pattern 3: Prahar Solar Clock Ragas
* **Observed in:** Classical Indian raga tradition; unique in casual mobile games.
* **Classification:** Potentially Distinctive
* **Purpose:** Aligns ambient lighting and musical modes with local solar time (Dawn, Noon, Evening, Night).
* **Potential Benefit:** Deepens connection with natural circadian rhythms and natural beauty.
* **Potential Implementation:** Real-time clock listener modulating Web Audio oscillator scales (Bhairav, Sarang, Yaman, Darbari).
* **Status:** **ADOPTED**

---

## Pattern 4: Paid "Extra Tray Slots" / Booster Shop
* **Observed in:** *Tile Club*, *Tile Busters*
* **Classification:** Common commercial mobile monetization pattern.
* **Purpose:** Selling an 8th tray slot or paid undos for real money.
* **Risk / Trade-off:** Coerces players through manufactured stress.
* **Status:** **REJECTED** (Replaced with free Prana Undo and voluntary Dakshina)
