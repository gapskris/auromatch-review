# Ethical Monetization & Sanctuary Patronage: AuroMatch

---

## 1. Ethical Manifesto: Pure Dignity in Matching

* Forcing full-screen video ads immediately after completing or failing a level.
* Charging real money (\$0.99 to \$4.99) for "Undo" or "Reshuffle" power-up packs.
* Placing fake banner timers that create manufactured stress.

**AuroMatch eliminates all commercial extortion.** The floral sanctuary is an inviolable space of calm, mental restoration, and natural beauty.

## 2. Core Ethical Guarantees

1. **0% Advertisements:** Never shows an advertisement, banner, or pop-up.
2. **Infinite Free Tools:** Undo, Auto-Sort, and Hint tools are 100% free and always available.
3. **No Energy Bars:** Play as much or as little as you desire; no artificial cooldown gates.
4. **Complete Offline Privacy:** No personal data, location tracking, or device profiling.
